## spare-02

**Claim 1** (finding): Status text in several documents falls behind what is deployed
- supports: `issue-210`, `issue-246`, `issue-285` — three separate incidents of stale documentation.

**Claim 2** (mechanism): The same status is written by hand in several places
- no evidence.

**Route: blog**

Claim 1 is a pattern seen across more than one incident (three separate cases of documentation drift). Claim 2 offers an explanation of why it happens, though it lacks evidence. The route description for **blog** requires a pattern together with an explanation of why it happens. The pattern is well supported; the mechanism is plausible but unevidenced. This is more than a single post, but not a measurement paper. The closest route is **blog**, though the missing evidence for the mechanism is a weakness.

## spare-04

**Claim 1** (finding): Two simultaneous requests to the model server are safe for memory
- supports: `issue-211-2026-09-30` (measurement): Swap did not move and the server kept the same process id.
- contradicts: `issue-211-2026-09-30-peak` (measurement): Wired memory reached about 25.8 GB on the 32 GB machine, closer to its limit than planned.

**Route: insufficient_evidence**

The claim is directly contradicted by another measurement. The supporting evidence is weak (swap did not move, same PID) against the contradicting evidence (memory near the limit). A **paper** requires a measurement that holds up when checked against a baseline and a control. Here the measurement does not hold up — it is contradicted. There is not enough consistent evidence for even a post, because the claim itself is disputed by the evidence. This is **insufficient_evidence**.
